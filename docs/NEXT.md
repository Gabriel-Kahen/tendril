# Completing the intended system

Updated September 27, 2026. The sole supported evolutionary path is Jev-guided selection: every evaluated candidate requires a valid Jev choice. There is no unguided/adaptive mode or random fallback. Stochastic legal proposal generation and parent sampling remain part of diversity-preserving evolution. Standalone physics fixtures and replay tools are not alternative search paths.

## Needed for a substantial guided run

1. **Continuous local feedback — implemented.** Regulatory state advances throughout juvenile and adult life, through tree and loop connections. Solver-active tissue/link contacts reach local controllers and growth rules. See FEEDBACK_MODEL.md for equations, ownership and tested limitations. Sensory modulation of emitted signals remains a possible explicit extension.
2. **Robust physical qualification.** Archive admission currently uses one primary rollout. Add repeated perturbations, refined timesteps, distributions and an explicit verification status before treating retained behaviors as discoveries. Tighten penetration relative to member thickness and quantify numerical energy error.
3. **Paid-run wiring — implemented.** Private local credential loading, pinned model, authenticated selection and usage reporting, request/cost admission gates, conservative reservations and explicit checkpoint resume are available. The wall deadline is checked between requests/batches and permits in-flight work to finish; strict mid-batch termination remains unimplemented. See LIVE_READINESS.md. Context overflow and provider errors stop for explicit resume without substitution.
4. **Measure sustained diversity and capability.** Run longer replicated guided experiments, review archive niches for trivial stationary variants, and retain passive developmental precursors. Jev selects variations; physical evidence and local archive competition continue to determine survival. Without an unguided comparison, do not claim Jev caused an advantage over it.

## Remaining product and body-development functionality

- **Run controls and observation:** start, checkpoint/pause/resume, configure budgets and inspect live progress through the app. Current gallery shows recorded states and refreshes completed specimens; CLI run/resume exists. Side-by-side specimen comparison, lineage navigation and clip export are not implemented.
- **Richer tissue development:** the present body can differentiate complete fixed-resolution elastic patches. Continuous tissue-volume growth, fusion, remeshing and material remodeling require new developmental operations with state/mass/energy transfer checks. These remain research proposals to implement deliberately, not capabilities supplied by the API key.

Repair/regeneration, ecosystems/metabolism and free-form generation of new module templates remain optional future directions. They are not required to turn on the intended Jev-guided developmental search.
